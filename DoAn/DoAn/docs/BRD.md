# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Goal

Develop an online shoe store that supports product browsing, online ordering, and store management.

## Business Objectives

- Improve customer shopping experience.
- Simplify product and order management.
- Provide a convenient online purchasing process.

## Business Processes

- Inventory Restocking
- User Registration
- Order Placement

## Stakeholders

- Customers
- Store Administrator

## Scope

### Customer

- Register
- Login
- Browse products
- Search products
- Manage shopping cart
- Place orders
- View order history

### Administrator

- Manage categories
- Manage products
- Manage customer orders
